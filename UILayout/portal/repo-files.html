Content/Maps

Persistent levels and sublevels for Ashfall. One folder per zone; the persistent level is <Zone>_P.umap.

Locks

  • Lock a .umap before you open it in the editor: lore lock Content/Maps/Frostreach/Frostreach_P.umap.
  • Release when you push. Locks held longer than 14 d are marked stale.
  • Lighting sublevels belong to the lighting team; ask aiko.tanaka before you take one.

Naming

Sublevels use <Zone>_<Layer>.umap, for example Frostreach_Audio.umap.
